The live campaign order places this after level 01 (display 2), whose powered lift and Gem bubble already teach a rider to use FLOAT and return to the lift, and level 03 (display 4), which teaches a held STOP across a motorized ferry crossing. Partner route-opening is introduced even earlier in level 02. The revision combines those ideas; level 28 (display 38) is later and is not a prior lesson here. Separate from the proven loading-position correction, the bounded Gem-floor probe moved Gem onto safe_floor by frame 199. Native completions at 1701/1727 frames and both browser clears with zero deaths support the authored route; the 1727 schedule corrects Pac's loading position and does not show a POWER overshoot. **The Liver-y Service** (`level_58`, display 39) sits between Two for One (`level_28`, display 38) and Special Delivery for Gem (`level_15`, display 40). Its required plan is to deliver the light sac and put both cells with it on WEIGH 3; the native traces activate the pad at weight 3. Earlier campaign exposure includes pushing a heavy sac onto a team pad and powering a ferry in level 09 (display 20), cargo-powered ferry/bridge relays in levels 12 and 23 (displays 25 and 23), and the partner lift and different bubble effects in level 28 immediately before this room. Level 15 is the next room and adds later practice with cargo weight and STEADY/RESCUE; it cannot be prior exposure. Level 58 recombines familiar cargo and villain interactions with a new WEIGH 3 threshold and moving layout. Pac can push the sac along the lower route; Isp can use the three static upper ledges and can draw Cana's nearest-player boomerang away. The `cana_fold` is a separate moving platform that Cana rides; the route does not require Isp to ride it. The hint uses "can" for both hero strategies, and the no-decoy route confirms Cana baiting is optional: the paired route clears in 765 frames, while the no-decoy route clears in 1130 and records Cana nudging the sac before recovery. Thus ignoring the decoy can disturb the cargo and take longer but does not block completion. The evidence supports KEEP; it demonstrates neither a cue defect nor need for hint polishing. Compared with 28's repeatable bubble route and no configured hazard, 58 adds Isp-only slime and parcel pressure; compared with the following 15, it uses a light rather than heavy parcel and asks for no STEADY/RESCUE relay. See the [live campaign order](../../../src/levels.ts#L98), [earlier cargo lessons](../../../src/levels_middle.ts#L277), [current level definition](../../../src/level_livery_service.ts#L18), `NATIVE_EVIDENCE.md`, and `PHASE_B_REPORT.md`. No human discovery or difficulty result follows from these prepared routes.

**Cana's Control Freak-Out** (`level_19`, display 57, before level 20 at 58) preserves the ferry opening and adds blue TARGET as bridge support before the final crossing. The mandatory relay is STOP, Pac reaches STEADY, Isp latches RESCUE, then Isp holds blue TARGET so Pac can cross and take orange TARGET; both hold their owner-specific targets to finish. Earlier levels taught ferry control, route latches, and villain-target interactions; the blue bridge-support handoff now has a clear immediate transfer into level 20's target-and-bridge sequence. That sequence being successfully performed does not establish that players learned or mastered the mechanic. Releasing STOP before the STEADY handoff can stall the crossing; a fall into either moat or Cana's core is lethal and costs the pair the relay. Cana remains active at the far finish after RESCUE and the blue TARGET; players must still deal with his attacks. That continuing threat can make the finish lively, but may conflict with an expectation that RESCUE neutralizes him. A late blue-target release/retake clear tests retaking after Pac reaches the target, not recovery from a missed crossing. Browser play cleared in 8.167 seconds with zero deaths; registered native routes cleared at 978 and 1000 frames. The old current57 profiles for 19 and 20 were both high-puzzle/high-platforming estimates, but predate these edits and must not be inherited as revised scores. See [source](../../../src/levels_final.ts#L320) and `PHASE_B_REPORT.md`.

The evidence here combines current definitions with selected native traces and bounded browser routes.
