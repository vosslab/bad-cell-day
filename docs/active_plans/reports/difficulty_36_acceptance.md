## Limits that remain in force

The rubric's scores are ordinal expert estimates. Idle captures, authored source, native probes, and
scripted routes do not measure human difficulty, novice completion, enjoyment, time, frustration,
learning, or two-person control comfort. Keep `null` values visible. Historical route coverage is
uneven; `no-reusable-trace` means no trace was located in the bounded inventory, not that a route is
impossible. No current 36-room browser journey, human study, novice discovery, completion rate,
enjoyment, frustration, or shared-keyboard comfort was measured. A native replay is not browser
acceptance, and previous-run findings are historical until checked against current36 evidence. Root
selects and authorizes a separate bounded lung-tissue compression/recoil prototype to launch a
partner toward a reciprocal return path, distinct from HOLD, cargo, and lift relays. The idea remains
unproven; the production decision follows evidence from the experiment. Set it aside if it does not
create a distinct cooperative choice. No new physics framework, configuration, or feature quota is
called for. A full campaign refresh is not a prerequisite for each future gameplay change.
