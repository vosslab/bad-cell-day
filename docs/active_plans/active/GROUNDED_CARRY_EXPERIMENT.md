# Grounded carry experiment

Status: private native feasibility probe complete; root selected KEEP with concerns and authorized bounded production/playtest as stable `level_39`, **Boneheads**. The production plan is [BONEHEADS_ROOM.md](BONEHEADS_ROOM.md). Native evidence remains a feasibility result, not public-source proof or human-readability acceptance.

Hypothesis: Pac's grounded walking can carry Isp to a high tissue contact that stabilizes a fragile marrow span, then Isp dismounts to an upper branch while Pac continues on the lower branch. The sustained contact should change Pac's viable route, making the carry itself useful rather than a boarding trick.

Overlap check: level 24 already has Pac riding FLOAT-capable Isp to hold CROSS while Isp uses the lower route; level 28 has a stacked jump and shared bubble; level 37 uses jointly supported recoil to launch a partner. The private probe differs by using Pac as grounded carrier, Isp as the moving high contact, and a collapsing span whose support depends on that contact. Root kept this bounded candidate with concerns; human readability and enjoyment remain unmeasured and are not gates to begin production.

Prototype choice: marrow. The canopy pad is fixed in world space and has no `platformId`; it stabilizes `carry_span` through the existing pad effect. It still detects Isp while the span is inactive, which avoids attached-pad occupancy suppression. No lower catch floor is present; a miss can fail and use ordinary Restart. The upper shelf and lower bank are the two post-handoff routes.

Probe evidence, exact commands, limitations, and the KEEP recommendation are in `tests/_temp/grounded_carry_puzzle_scout/report/REPORT.md`. Root owns the private result. Root's production authorization and bounded acceptance gates are recorded in [BONEHEADS_ROOM.md](BONEHEADS_ROOM.md).
